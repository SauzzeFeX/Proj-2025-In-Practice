Sept. 16, 2026 [ S. 32 ]   Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representa­tives of the United States of America in Congress assembled, Local Access to Courts Act. SECTION 1. 28 USC 1 note . SHORT TITLE.   This Act may be cited as the “ Local Access to Courts Act ” or “ LACA ”. SEC. 2. ORGANIZATION OF TEXAS DISTRICT COURTS.    Section 124(b)(2) of title 28, United States Code , is amended , in the matter preceding paragraph (3), by inserting “ and College Station ” before the period at the end. SEC. 3. ORGANIZATION OF CALIFORNIA DISTRICT COURTS.    Section 84(d) of title 28, United States Code , is amended by inserting “ and El Centro ” after “ at San Diego ”. Approved September 16, 2026 .
