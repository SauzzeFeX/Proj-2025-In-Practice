Oct. 20, 2026 [ H.R. 7831 ]   Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representa­tives of the United States of America in Congress assembled, License to Drill Act. SECTION 1. 30 USC 181 note . SHORT TITLE.   This Act may be cited as the “ License to Drill Act ”. SEC. 2. BLM OIL AND GAS PERMIT PROCESSING FEE.   Section 35(d) of the Mineral Leasing Act ( 30 U.S.C. 191(d) ) is amended — (1) in paragraph (1), by striking “ 2026 ” and inserting “ 2037 ”; (2) in paragraph (3)— (A) by striking “ Of the fees collected under this subsection for a fiscal year, the ” and inserting “ The ”; and (B) by striking “ transfer— ” and all that follows through “ the Fund ” and inserting “ transfer, for each of fiscal years 2027 through 2037, all of the fees collected under this subsection for each such fiscal year to the BLM Permit Processing Improvement Fund ”; and (3) in paragraph (4), by striking “ 2026 ” and inserting “ 2037 ”.
